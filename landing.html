What is Beamax?

Beamax isn't just a black-box calculator. It is a full engineering environment that uses exact mathematical methods to solve complex continuous beams. Whether you are verifying a manual calculation for a class assignment or quickly checking a design for a real project, Beamax gives you the exact bending moments, shear forces, deflections, and RC reinforcement details you need.

Benefit

Stop Guessing, Start Knowing

Most software spits out a final answer, leaving you wondering how it got there. Beamax acts like a digital tutor. It renders every calculation step-by-step using beautifully formatted LaTeX equations. You can see exactly which numbers are plugged into the formulas, making it incredibly easy to understand the mechanics behind the solution.

Everything in One Workspace

Feature

64-Bit Direct Stiffness Method

At the core of Beamax is a robust Finite Element Method (FEM) solver. It automatically breaks your continuous beam down into nodes and elements, assembles the global stiffness matrix, and solves for exact nodal displacements and rotations. This handles any combination of point loads, distributed loads, and support types flawlessly.

Feature

Exact Algebraic Root Finding

We don't settle for graphical approximations. When Beamax calculates your maximum bending moments, it uses a 1-millimeter micro-sweep resolution to find the exact algebraic peak where shear force equals zero. This ensures your design forces are mathematically perfect, not just "close enough."

Feature

Classic Solvers Built-In

Need to verify a homework assignment? Beamax includes dedicated analytical solvers for the Three Moment Equation and Fixed-End Moments. It generates the exact compatibility equations, shows the matrix setup, and solves them step-by-step, just like you would on paper.

Feature

Double Integration for Deflection

Visualize exactly how your beam will bend under load. The app performs double integration of the bending moment equations to plot highly accurate deflection curves. It instantly highlights the maximum deflection point and warns you if the deflection exceeds standard serviceability limits (like L/250).

New Module

Complete BS 8110 RC Design

Seamlessly transition from structural analysis to concrete design. The built-in Design Module extracts the exact critical envelope moments and shear forces from your beam. You simply specify your concrete and steel grades, cover, and section dimensions, and Beamax handles the rest in accordance with British Standards.

Smart Suggestion

Automatic Bar & Link Selection

You define your preferred Main Bar diameter (e.g., 16mm) and Link diameter (e.g., 10mm). Beamax calculates the required reinforcement area, checks it against strict code minimums, and outputs practical, real-world bar configurations (like "Provide 3Y16") and exact link spacings.
